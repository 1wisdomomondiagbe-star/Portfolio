# 05 — Process

### Section label

**04 / How it comes together**

### Section headline

**The work gets**

**better in motion.**

---

## Step 01 — Listen

Find the signal inside the brief and understand what needs to be felt.

I begin with the idea, the audience, the context, and the outcome. The goal is to understand what the work needs to do before deciding what it needs to look like.

---

## Step 02 — Explore

Make room for references, rough cuts, strange ideas, and new directions.

This is where possibilities open up. I explore visual references, compositions, pacing, tools, textures, and different creative routes until the strongest direction begins to emerge.

---

## Step 03 — Shape

Turn the strongest idea into a clear, crafted, considered experience.

The selected direction becomes a refined piece of work. Every frame, word, color, transition, and interaction is brought into alignment.

---

## Step 04 — Release

Put it into the world with enough confidence to let it move people.

The finished work should be ready to live in the real world: clear enough to understand, strong enough to remember, and flexible enough to keep working after launch.
